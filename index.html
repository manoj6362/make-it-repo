FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long does a full project take?

Most studio engagements run six to eight weeks from kickoff to launch. Sprints land in ten working days. We share a dated plan before anything starts.

Do you work with existing brand guidelines?

Often. We can extend and modernise a system you already own, or build one from first principles. Either way you receive full source files and documentation.

Which platforms do you build on?

Framer, Webflow, Next.js and bespoke front-ends. The choice follows your team’s ability to maintain it, never our preference.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of complimentary support, then an optional partner retainer for ongoing design, iteration and performance work.
